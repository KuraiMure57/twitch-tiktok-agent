Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Twitch TikTok Agent handles information when the application is used by its owner.

1. Information Processed

Depending on the enabled functionality, the application may process video files, audio, subtitles, generated metadata and authentication information required to interact with connected third-party services.

2. TikTok Information

When TikTok integration is enabled, the application may process authentication credentials and information necessary to publish videos to the connected TikTok account.

TikTok data is used only for the functionality requested by the account owner, such as publishing approved videos.

3. Video and Audio Processing

Gaming videos and their audio may be processed to identify moments, generate subtitles, create metadata and prepare short-form videos.

4. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services including Twitch, TikTok, Telegram, GitHub and Google Gemini.

Each third-party service may process information according to its own privacy policy.

5. Data Storage

The application is designed primarily for personal use. Processed files and authentication information may be stored temporarily or in the connected infrastructure required to operate the service.

6. Data Sharing

The application does not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to connected third-party services when necessary to provide the requested functionality.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and application data. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or integrations change.

9. Contact

For questions regarding privacy or this application, contact the application owner through the associated GitHub project.
